Solutions

Capabilities for the build floor

Industrial proof-of-life for cable drops that must sleep — fleet visibility without draining batteries or depending on inbound ping.

01

Fleet visibility

Hang one module per far-end drop. Your collector reports ok, late, or missing — not guesswork on the floor.

02

Battery for months

LiPo-powered deep sleep for multi-month construction where PoE is not yet available at the far end.

03

No ICMP dependency

Outbound HTTP phone-home only. Modules are not pingable by design — the receive path is the POST response.

Scheduled wake

Timer wakes ~every 15 minutes for a short heartbeat window, then returns to deep sleep.

Technology

Sleep. Wake. Prove it. Sleep.

A deliberate cycle for multi-month builds — not always-on monitoring.

  1. 01

    Deep sleep

    Radio and MCU off. No listeners. Battery conserved for the long haul of the build schedule.

  2. 02

    Wake + DHCP

    Timer fires (~every 15 minutes). Ethernet comes up; the module obtains an address on the plant network.

  3. 03

    POST heartbeat

    JSON status — unit id, battery, MAC, and more — POSTed to your collector endpoint.

  4. 04

    Confirm & sleep

    Wait for HTTP 2xx (up to three tries), then return to deep sleep until the next wake.
