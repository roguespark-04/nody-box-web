Product

Proof of life for drops that must sleep

Keeping a module online for ICMP would drain the pack over a multi-month build. Nody Box phones home on its own schedule so your collector sees which drops are still alive while cabling continues.

Scheduled wake, not always-on

Deep sleep conserves battery. Timer wakes the module about every 15 minutes for a short heartbeat window.

Not pingable — by design

No inbound ICMP or listeners. The only receive path is the HTTP response to the module’s own POST.

Battery for the build

LiPo-powered for months-long construction where PoE is not yet available at the far end of the run.

Fleet visibility

Hang one on each far-end drop. Your collector reports ok, late, or missing — not guesswork on the floor.

On-device OLED

Glance at unit id, battery voltage, and last confirmation at the rack — no laptop required for a quick check.

Wired plant, USB provision

W5500 Ethernet at the drop. Fleet provisioning over USB with etctl; native field apps coming soon.

How it works

Sleep. Wake. Prove it. Sleep.

A deliberate cycle for multi-month builds — not always-on monitoring.

  1. 01

    Deep sleep

    Radio and MCU off. No listeners. Battery conserved for the long haul of the build schedule.

  2. 02

    Wake + DHCP

    Timer fires (~every 15 minutes). Ethernet comes up; the module obtains an address on the plant network.

  3. 03

    POST heartbeat

    JSON status — unit id, battery, MAC, and more — POSTed to your collector endpoint.

  4. 04

    Confirm & sleep

    Wait for HTTP 2xx (up to three tries), then return to deep sleep until the next wake.
