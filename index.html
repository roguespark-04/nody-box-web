How it works

Sleep. Wake. Prove it. Sleep.

A simple cycle designed for multi-month builds — not for always-on monitoring.

  1. 01

    Deep sleep

    Radio and MCU off. No listeners. Battery conserved for the long haul.

  2. 02

    Wake + DHCP

    Timer fires (~every 15 minutes). Ethernet comes up; the module takes an address.

  3. 03

    POST heartbeat

    JSON status — id, battery, MAC, and more — POSTed to your collector.

  4. 04

    Confirm & sleep

    Wait for HTTP 2xx (up to 3 tries), then drop back into deep sleep.

Why it exists

You can’t ping what must sleep.

Keeping a module online for ICMP would kill the pack over a multi-month build. Nody Box phones home on its own schedule instead — so you know which drops are still alive while cabling continues.

  • Not pingable — on purpose

    Inbound traffic isn’t served. The sole receive path is the HTTP response to its own POST.

  • Battery for the build

    Designed to last through months of construction without PoE or constant power.

  • Fleet visibility

    Hang one on the far end of each run. Your collector sees ok / late / missing — not guesswork.

  • Built for AI infra build-outs

    For datacenter construction and network engineers who need proof of life, not a sketch pad.

Hardware · v0

Small board. Long memory.

Compact, battery-first hardware for the cable plant — provisioned over USB with etctl.

MCU

ESP32

Low-power MCU for wake, sleep, and the heartbeat cycle.

ETH

W5500 Ethernet

Wired link at the far end of the run — no Wi‑Fi dependency in the plant.

BAT

LiPo (not PoE)

Self-powered for months-long builds where PoE isn’t in place yet.

OLED

OLED display

On-device glance at id, battery, and last confirmation — no laptop required.
